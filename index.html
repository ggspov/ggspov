What We Do

One bag.
One life changed.

Every GGS POV care bag is assembled by hand with love and distributed directly to individuals experiencing homelessness — in South Florida and beyond.

🎒

Hygiene Care Bags

Soap, shampoo, deodorant, toothbrush, feminine products, socks, underwear, food — every bag assembled by hand and delivered with love and dignity.

🌍

Global Vision

We serve homeless individuals and families throughout South Florida, across the United States, and internationally — wherever the need exists.

🙏

Faith in Action

Rooted in faith, driven by compassion. Every bag is prepared with prayer and the belief that every human life has infinite worth and dignity.

Our Story

It started with a quiet calling.

For years, founder Heidi Gonzalez assembled hygiene bags for homeless men and women — not for recognition, not for reward, but as a private act of faith. No cameras. No followers. Just love in action.

Today, GGS POV, Inc. is formalizing that mission to grow its impact — inviting the world to join in God's work, one bag at a time.
